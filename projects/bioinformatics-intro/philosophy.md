# Production Philosophy: The Exciting World of Bioinformatics

## Visual Identity
- **Art Style**: Cinematic documentary realism, grounded academic realism, warm 35mm film photography aesthetic. Natural skin texture, subtle film grain, believable study environment without glossy CGI artificiality.
- **Color Palette**: Deep maroon, warm ivory, muted earthy greens, charcoal, soft natural daylight with amber room accents.
- **Lighting**: Soft diffused side window light supplemented by a warm desk lamp. Natural soft shadows, authentic dorm/study atmosphere.
- **Composition**: Intimate medium close-ups (chest-up talking head) adhering to rule of thirds, subtle depth of field with soft bookshelf and paper collage background bokeh.

## Motion Language
- **Movement Quality**: Organic, fluid, human micro-movements. Natural head tilts, expressive eyebrows, realistic blinking, subtle hand gestures emphasizing scientific points.
- **Pacing**: Conversational, engaging, thoughtful rhythm. Natural pauses that mirror authentic speaking and reasoning.
- **Camera Style**: Subtle handheld breathing motion / gentle locked-off conversational portrait angle. No erratic pans or artificial zoom snaps.

## Subject Consistency (ANGSUMI)
- **Character**: Young Northeast Indian PhD scholar in life sciences (Assamese roots). Dark brown hair worn in an effortless loose bun with textured wisps framing her face. Expressive almond-shaped eyes, natural warm smile, authentic skin tones.
- **Wardrobe**: Authentic cozy deep maroon knit sweater with subtle earthy patterned knit detailing across chest and shoulders (consistent with reference image).
- **Environment**: Lived-in academic scholar room: wooden bookshelf filled with molecular biology textbooks, small potted indoor pothos plant, bulletin board with field notes, research posters, and scientific diagrams.
- **Props**: Laptop displaying bioinformatic scripts (Python/R terminal and 3D molecular protein structures), notebook with ballpoint pen.

## Constraints
- **Avoid**: Flawless airbrushed plastic skin, sensationalized clickbait expressions, robotic stiff mouth movement, exaggerated anime expressions, tourism-costume caricatures, fake high-tech holograms.
- **Maintain**: Believable PhD scholar authenticity, realistic lighting matching the reference photo, grounded scientific curiosity, consistent maroon sweater and hairstyle across all scenes.
