# ANGSUMI project instructions

## What this folder is

This is a brand and content planning workspace, not a software repository. It contains a creator persona and brand plan, a niche research report, a filled identity kit, an October 2026 content calendar, and visual references/campaign drafts. Do not assume there is an app to build or a test suite to run.

## Brand foundation

- ANGSUMI is a **fictional** young Northeast Indian PhD scholar and researcher-builder. The persona document places her in life sciences at Mizoram University, with roots in Assam. Do not present fictional biography, credentials, research, or results as verified real-world facts.
- Positioning: **“The Researcher Who Builds.”** Core subjects are bioinformatics and research data, coding for researchers, and real PhD life. The promise is to help life-science researchers understand, analyze, and communicate their data.
- Audience: mainly Indian life-science postgraduate students, PhD scholars, research assistants, and early-career researchers. Explain technical concepts for capable beginners without talking down to them.
- Voice: curious, clear, grounded, practical. Use precise English scientific terms with natural English-dominant Hinglish where it helps. Show reasoning, limitations, mistakes, and progress. Avoid fake expertise, inflated outcomes, forced clickbait, generic motivation, and constant selling.
- Keep science accurate. Explain what a plot, statistic, or workflow can and cannot establish. Use realistic research outputs and cite/check sources for scientific or time-sensitive claims. Never invent datasets, experimental results, testimonials, services delivered, or publication success.

## Content decisions

- Use bioinformatics/research data as the authority anchor; researcher-focused coding as the utility and growth bridge; PhD life as the human layer. The niche report proposes a **50/30/20** split for a first 12-week test. The earlier persona plan uses a more detailed six-pillar split; treat that as a source of subtopics, not a competing mandate.
- Teach from a recognizable research problem toward interpretation or a usable workflow. Prefer “what does this PCA mean?” or “why automate this lab spreadsheet?” over generic software lessons.
- Default short-form structure: hook in the first 1–2 seconds, one clear problem, visual evidence or demonstration, one useful explanation, and one CTA. Make on-screen text readable without sound.
- The strategy report recommends three strong vertical pieces per week plus a supporting post, with cross-posting to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels. The October workbook instead lists **62 planned posts, two per day**. Treat its rows as planned ideas, not proof of publication or an automatic production commitment. Confirm cadence when scheduling work.
- Assess content using retention, saves, shares, relevant comments, follows, and qualified inquiries as well as views. Do not claim performance without actual platform analytics.
- Ethical commercial directions include education, analysis support, scientific visualization, reproducible workflows, and training. Do not offer ghostwriting, fabricated analysis, or work that substitutes for a researcher's academic responsibility.

## Visual direction and asset handling

- Core palette in the persona plan: warm ivory, deep maroon, charcoal, muted green, restrained earthy accents. Campaign designs use editorial serif headlines and generous space; everyday content should feel more documentary and human.
- Character references show a believable young Assamese/Northeast Indian woman with realistic skin texture, dark hair, simple practical clothing, and ordinary study/lab/field settings. Keep appearance consistent within a sequence. Use regional details as lived context, not costume or tourism decoration.
- The PNGs include character sheets, portraits, campaign posters, service graphics, and simple logo/brand cards. Several posters feature other people, community stories, or a male figure; do not assume every pictured person is ANGSUMI. Inspect the particular image before reusing or editing it.
- Preserve originals. Create clearly named derivatives when generating or adapting assets, and check dimensions/crop and legibility before delivery.

- `brand-and-strategy/ANGSUMI Niche Validation & Content Strategy for India.docx`: market hypothesis, positioning, 12-week experiment, topic ideas, and measurement approach. Its market snapshots and numerical claims are dated research, so recheck them for current use.
- `brand-and-strategy/ANGSUMI_Creator_Identity_Kit_FILLED.pdf`: compact creator identity and three-pillar reference.
- `content-planning/ANGSUMI_October_2026_Content_Calendar.xlsx`: dated editorial plan and dashboard; status cells are planned, not publication evidence.

- `outputs/`: carousel images and their `captions.txt` files; Facebook carousel 30 remains queued for scheduling on 9 October 2026.

For a new request, follow the user's current brief first, then the relevant source file. If sources disagree, state the specific difference and choose the direction that fits the current deliverable. Preserve source files unless the user asks to edit them.
