Before interpreting a heatmap, check what its colors represent.

Normalized expression and row z-scores answer different questions. Row scaling shows each gene relative to its own mean across samples; it does not show absolute expression differences between genes.

Also check the gene selection rule, sample annotations, and clustering settings. A visible cluster is a starting point for interpretation.
