Bioinformatics starts with a biological question.

Before opening a tool, ask: What was measured? Which samples are being compared? What does the study design allow me to conclude?

This first case file follows a simple route: question → biological sample → measured data → quality checks → analysis → interpretation. A clean-looking plot is evidence to examine; it does not prove a biological explanation on its own.
