Renaming .txt to .fastq does not convert your file.

The extension suggests a format. It does not prove the structure inside. FASTA: sequence records. FASTQ: sequence and per-base quality. CSV commonly uses commas. TSV uses tabs. Check delimiters and quoting. Inspect a small example. Choose a tool that supports its actual format.

Check the inside before uploading.  Save: rename ≠ convert.
