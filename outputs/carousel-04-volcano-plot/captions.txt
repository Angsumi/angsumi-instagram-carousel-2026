A volcano plot is easier to read when you know the comparison, axes, and coloring rules.

The horizontal axis shows estimated log2 fold change for the chosen contrast. The vertical axis often shows minus log10 of a p-value or adjusted p-value. Check the label rather than assuming they are interchangeable.

The example uses adjusted p < 0.05 and |log2FC| > 1 to highlight genes. These are illustrative criteria, not a universal rule for every study.

Then look beyond the colored dots: inspect sample-level expression, uncertainty, and biological context.
