## Caption

Bioinformatics begins with a biological question.

Before choosing a tool, ask what you measured, what comparison matters, and what your study design can support.

Question → Data → Quality checks → Analysis → Interpretation.

Code helps you carry out the workflow. Understanding the biology gives the workflow its direction.

Save this map before starting your next analysis.
