Do you need to code before you can analyse biological data?

A GUI gives you visual controls. A CLI accepts typed commands. Both can support serious work.

FastQC is one example of a tool with graphical and command-line interfaces. Galaxy offers a browser workspace with histories and reusable workflows, including options for repeated analyses. Your results depend on the tool, version, inputs and settings—not how impressive the screen looks.

Keep your command or workflow, input files, reference versions, settings and outputs together. The interfaces shown here are illustrations; no analysis was run for these cards.
